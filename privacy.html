Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

PeakCut is a personal tool used by its developer to upload their own video clips to their own TikTok account.

What we collect

When you connect TikTok, PeakCut receives an access token and your basic profile information (display name). This is used only to upload videos you choose to post.

How data is stored

Tokens are stored locally on the computer running PeakCut. They are not sold, shared, or sent to any third party.

Deleting your data

You can disconnect TikTok inside PeakCut at any time, or revoke access in TikTok under Settings, Security, Manage app permissions. Disconnecting deletes the stored token.
